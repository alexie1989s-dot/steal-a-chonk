# Steal a Chonk

An original Roblox steal game about enormous, round, sleepy animals called **Chonks**. Sneak into the field, grab a blanket burrito from a guarded nest, outrun the grumpy Mama Chonk back to your house, and unwrap whatever was inside. Train your **Zoomies** on the hamster wheel to run faster and reach farther zones. Built mobile-first, with affectionate humour.

> **The playable prototype is on another branch.** `main` holds the design and the early art work. The greybox prototype lives on [`prototype/vertical-slice`](https://github.com/alexie1989s-dot/steal-a-chonk/tree/prototype/vertical-slice) and hasn't been merged here yet. Its README explains how to play it, what to test and what's next.

## Where we are (updated 25 Sep 2026)

- **Design:** the full [design spec](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-steal-a-chonk-design.md) is written: 26 sections, still marked draft for both of us to review.
- **Prototype:** a playable greybox of the core loop (grab, chase, carry home, unwrap, earn) is built and tested solo, on `prototype/vertical-slice`. Two-player and phone tests are still to do.
- **Art is paused** while we get the prototype right. The corgi model here is a rejected early attempt, kept for reference.
- **Next:** the Showcase heist. You'll be able to unwrap Chonks on your porch for more income, and steal from other players' porches. It is planned on the prototype branch.
